Antidiuretic hormone and thirst respond to hypothalamic osmoreceptor, arterial baroreceptor and
angiotensin II signals.

Relevant direct local physiological relationships:

- plasma_osmolality increases ADH.
- plasma_osmolality increases thirst.
- arterial_pressure reduces ADH.
- angiotensin_II increases ADH.
- angiotensin_II increases thirst.
