The kidney responds to arterial pressure, sympathetic activity, aldosterone and ADH, regulating
renal perfusion, glomerular filtration, urine formation and renin release.

Relevant direct local physiological relationships:

- arterial_pressure increases renal_perfusion.
- renal_perfusion increases GFR.
- GFR increases urine_output.
- renal_perfusion reduces renin.
- sympathetic_activity increases renin.
- aldosterone reduces urine_output.
- ADH reduces urine_output.
