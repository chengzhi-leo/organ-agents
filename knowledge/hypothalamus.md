# Hypothalamus

## Local Dynamics

### Osmotic and Volume Regulation

Hypothalamic osmoreceptors respond to changes in extracellular osmolality.

Increased plasma osmolality activates hypothalamic neurons involved in thirst and antidiuretic hormone regulation.

Signals associated with reduced effective circulating volume can also promote hypothalamic activation of water-conserving responses.

### Energy and Nutrient Sensing

The hypothalamus integrates glucose, nutrient-related signals, and hormones reflecting energy stores.

Leptin and other metabolic signals influence hypothalamic circuits involved in appetite, energy expenditure, and metabolic regulation.

Changes in nutrient availability can modify hypothalamic autonomic and endocrine activity.

### Neuroendocrine Regulation

Hypothalamic neurons synthesize releasing and inhibiting hormones that regulate anterior pituitary secretion.

Major hypothalamic regulatory hormones include:

* corticotropin-releasing hormone
* thyrotropin-releasing hormone
* gonadotropin-releasing hormone
* growth-hormone-releasing hormone
* somatostatin
* dopamine

Feedback from circulating target-gland hormones can modify hypothalamic hormone production and release.

### ADH and Oxytocin Production

Specialized hypothalamic neurons synthesize antidiuretic hormone and oxytocin.

Their axons project to the posterior pituitary, where these hormones can subsequently be released into the circulation.

ADH synthesis and neuronal activity increase particularly in response to increased osmolality and signals associated with reduced circulating volume.

### Autonomic Regulation

The hypothalamus can modify sympathetic and parasympathetic activity through projections to autonomic control centers.

These responses participate in regulation of cardiovascular activity, thermoregulation, digestion, metabolism, and other visceral functions.

### Thermoregulation

Hypothalamic neurons integrate central and peripheral temperature-related signals.

Changes in body temperature can activate neural responses that promote heat production, heat conservation, or heat loss.

### Circadian Regulation

Hypothalamic circadian networks coordinate daily rhythms in endocrine, autonomic, behavioral, and metabolic activity.

Circadian signals can modify the timing of hormone release, sleep–wake behavior, body temperature, and other physiological processes.

### Stress Regulation

Physiological and psychological stress can activate hypothalamic neuroendocrine and autonomic pathways.

Corticotropin-releasing hormone secretion and sympathetic activity can increase during stress, coordinating endocrine and neural stress responses.
