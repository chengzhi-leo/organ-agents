The heart responds to circulating blood volume, venous return, cardiac filling and sympathetic
activity, and ejects blood into the arterial circulation.

Relevant direct local physiological relationships:

- blood_volume increases venous_return.
- venous_return increases cardiac_filling.
- cardiac_filling increases stroke_volume.
- contractility increases stroke_volume.
- stroke_volume increases cardiac_output.
- heart_rate increases cardiac_output.
- sympathetic_activity increases heart_rate.
- sympathetic_activity increases contractility.
