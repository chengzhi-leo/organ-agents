The vasculature responds to cardiac output, sympathetic activity, angiotensin II and ADH, setting
vascular tone, systemic vascular resistance and arterial pressure.

Relevant direct local physiological relationships:

- cardiac_output increases arterial_pressure.
- sympathetic_activity increases vascular_tone.
- angiotensin_II increases vascular_tone.
- ADH increases vascular_tone.
- vascular_tone increases vascular_resistance.
- vascular_resistance increases arterial_pressure.
